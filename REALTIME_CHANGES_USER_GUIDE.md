# Real-Time Parameter Changes - User Guide 🎵

## What Is This Feature?

You can now change music parameters (like voicing patterns, tempo, volume, etc.) **WHILE THE MUSIC IS PLAYING** and have those changes applied smoothly at the perfect moment—no more stopping and restarting!

## ✅ What Can Be Changed in Real-Time?

### HIGH PRIORITY (Changes Apply Smoothly)
- **Voicing Patterns**: Block ➜ Arpeggiated ➜ Strum ➜ Broken ➜ Partial
- **Pattern Variations**: Different strum patterns, arpeggio sequences, etc.
- **Tempo/BPM**: Speed up or slow down the music
- **Volume**: Adjust music and voice volume on the fly
- **Audio Effects**: Reverb, chorus, delay settings

### MEDIUM PRIORITY (Works Great Too!)
- **Octave Range**: Shift voicing up or down
- **Voicing Styles**: Drop 2, Close, Open voicing
- **Dynamics**: Accent patterns and velocity
- **Instrument**: Switch between piano, organ, strings, etc. (with visual feedback)

## 🎮 How To Use It

### Step 1: Open Real-Time Settings
Look for the **"Real-Time Parameter Changes"** panel in the Music Machine section.

### Step 2: Choose When Changes Apply
You have 4 options (pick what feels best for you):

#### ⚡ **Immediately**
- Changes happen RIGHT NOW
- ⚠️ May cause a brief audio "glitch" or discontinuity
- **Best for**: Experimenting quickly

#### ⏭️ **Next Chord** (Smooth)
- Changes happen when the next chord plays
- ✅ Smooth transition, no gaps
- **Best for**: Keeping the rhythm going

#### 🔄 **Next Loop** ⭐ **RECOMMENDED**
- Changes happen when the chord progression loops back to the beginning
- ✅ Most predictable and musical
- ✅ You hear the full progression before the change
- **Best for**: Most musical results

#### ⏹️ **Manual**
- Changes ONLY happen when you click STOP, then PLAY again
- Like the old way the app worked
- **Best for**: If you prefer full control

### Step 3: Make Your Changes
With the music playing, just click any voicing pattern, change the tempo, adjust volumes, etc.

### Step 4: Watch the Magic ✨
- A **badge appears** in the bottom-right showing your queued changes
- When the right moment arrives, changes apply **automatically**
- You get a **green notification** confirming it worked

## 💡 Pro Tips

### Tip #1: Start with "Next Loop"
This is the most musical option. Make changes, let the progression finish, and hear your new settings start fresh.

### Tip #2: Cancel Queued Changes
Changed your mind? Click the **X** button in the queued changes badge to cancel any change before it applies.

### Tip #3: Turn It Off If You Want
Toggle the Real-Time Changes **OFF** to go back to the original behavior (stop/play to change).

### Tip #4: Experiment Live!
Try switching from Block to Strum mid-song, or change arpeggio patterns—it's like having a DJ controller for your chord progressions!

## 🎯 Common Scenarios

### Scenario 1: "I want to try different voicing patterns"
1. Set timing to **"Next Loop"**
2. Click different patterns (Block, Arp, Strum, etc.)
3. Watch them queue up in the badge
4. When the loop restarts, hear your new pattern

### Scenario 2: "The music is too fast/slow"
1. Set timing to **"Immediately"** or **"Next Chord"**
2. Adjust the BPM slider or tempo dropdown
3. Change applies smoothly

### Scenario 3: "I want to build up the arrangement"
1. Start with just **Block** chords
2. After one loop, switch to **Broken** (adds movement)
3. After another loop, switch to **Strum** (full texture)
4. It's like arranging a song in real-time!

## 🎨 Visual Indicators

### What the Colors Mean:
- 🔵 **Blue dot**: "Will apply on next loop"
- 🟢 **Green dot**: "Will apply on next chord"
- 🟡 **Yellow badge**: "X changes queued"
- ✅ **Green flash**: "Change applied successfully!"

### The Queued Changes Badge:
- Shows in **bottom-right corner**
- Click to **expand** and see all queued changes
- Click **X** on individual items to cancel
- Click **trash icon** to cancel all

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### "My changes aren't applying"
- ✅ Check if Real-Time Changes is **ON**
- ✅ Make sure music is **PLAYING** (not stopped)
- ✅ If set to "Next Loop", wait for the loop to complete
- ✅ Check the queued changes badge to see if it's queued

### "Changes are jarring/glitchy"
- Switch from "Immediately" to **"Next Loop"** or **"Next Chord"**
- These timing modes ensure smooth, musical transitions

### "I don't see the settings panel"
- Scroll down in the Music Machine section
- Look for the **gear icon** and "Real-Time Parameter Changes"

### "Badge is blocking something"
- Click the badge to **minimize** it
- You can still make changes with it minimized

## ⚙️ Advanced: Option C Explained

**Option C** means YOU decide when changes apply. This feature gives you a setting (the 4 timing modes) so you can customize the behavior to match your workflow:

- Want instant feedback? Choose **Immediately**
- Want smooth transitions? Choose **Next Chord**
- Want predictable, musical changes? Choose **Next Loop**
- Want full manual control? Choose **Manual**

The system remembers your choice, so it works the way YOU want it to work.

## 🎓 Behind the Scenes

This system uses a **parameter queue** that:
1. Captures your changes while music plays
2. Waits for the right moment (chord/loop boundary)
3. Applies changes smoothly without interrupting audio
4. Gives you visual feedback every step of the way

It's like having a professional audio engineer smoothly transitioning your live performance!
